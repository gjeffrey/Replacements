# Icon — version 0.3.0

## Version 0.3.0 icon

The current asset is a sculptural cobalt-blue ribbon opening into three text bars on a porcelain-white tile. Generated and refined with the built-in image generation tool, then converted to standard icon sizes with sips/iconutil. It was inspected at 128 pixels and in the app sidebar. No existing icon or Apple assets were copied.

### Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final production macOS application icon for Replacements, a refined native utility for expanding a few keystrokes into phrases.
Primary request: a noticeably more remarkable, ownable icon that still looks like a beautifully crafted system utility. Design a single original sculptural symbol, not a generic flat toolbar glyph.
Subject: a broad continuous cobalt-blue ribbon folded into a compact rightward expansion mark. At its left it begins as a small rounded rectangular tab; the ribbon sweeps in one confident curving fold to the right, opening into three neatly stacked elongated rounded text bars. Integrate tab, fold and three bars into one coherent solid sculptural object, like a precisely manufactured typographic mechanism. The shortest bar is above the two longer bars. Use generous negative space and a strong memorable silhouette, readable at 32px.
Scene/backdrop: centered on a straight-on softly rounded square of warm porcelain-white with a whisper of silver shading. The blue ribbon floats a very short distance above the tile with soft local contact shadows. Subtle top-left light reveals beveled edges and satin translucent-blue depth, small hints of indigo in folded surfaces. Restrained, tactile and premium, not shiny toy plastic.
Composition: square 1024px canvas, rounded tile filling 88 percent of canvas, optically balanced symbol filling about 65 percent of tile. True transparent margin outside the tile. Clean alpha, perfectly smooth contour and subtle antialiasing.
Constraints: ONE icon only; no wordmark, no letters, no labels, no Apple logo, no copied icon, no sparkle, no rainbow, no checkerboard, no border, no loose specks or shadow outside the tile. No busy details. Flat straight-on tile; the blue folded symbol has subtle dimensionality.

### Refinement prompt

Edit this macOS application icon. Keep the distinctive cobalt-blue folded ribbon expansion symbol, its exact shape, its three text bars, the porcelain-white rounded-square tile, the proportions and restrained material lighting. Change ONLY the outer alpha contour: make the tile's rounded-square outline smooth and exact, removing every fringe, jagged speck, white scratch or fragment outside it, especially along the top, side and bottom edges. Entire tile and blue ribbon are fully opaque. Everything outside the tile is genuinely transparent. No diffuse external shadow, no loose pixels, no checkerboard. Preserve the artwork inside the tile. Production app-icon quality at small sizes.
